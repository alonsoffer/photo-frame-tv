Privacy Policy — Photo Frame

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Photo Frame ("the app") turns an Android TV into a digital photo frame and lets a companion phone manage the photos it shows. This policy explains what the app accesses, why, and what it does not do with your data.

What the app accesses

  • Google Drive (only the app's own folder). The app uses the drive.file permission. This grant lets the app read and manage only the files it creates itself — the photos you add through Photo Frame, kept in a single "Photo Frame" folder. The app cannot see, read, or access any other file in your Google Drive.
  • Photos you add. When you add photos from your phone, they are uploaded to that "Photo Frame" folder in your own Google Drive and displayed on your TV. They stay in your Drive; you can delete them at any time from the app or from Google Drive directly.
  • Camera (phone only, optional). The camera is used for one purpose: scanning the QR code shown on your TV to pair it. Camera images are processed on the device to read the code and are never stored or transmitted.

What is stored, and where

  • A Google sign-in token (a "refresh token") is stored locally on your device only, so the app can keep showing your photos without asking you to sign in every time. It is never sent to us or to any third party.
  • Your app settings (such as language, transition style, and refresh interval) are stored locally on your device.

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not collect, sell, or share your personal data with any third party.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • It has no server of its own — your photos go directly between your device and your own Google Drive.
  • It does not access any Google Drive file other than the ones it created.

Google API Services disclosure

Photo Frame's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect the app at any time from within Photo Frame, which deletes the stored token from your device. You can also revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access does not delete photos already in your Drive; delete those from Google Drive if you wish.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
