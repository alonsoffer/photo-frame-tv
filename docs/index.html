Photo Frame

Turn your Google TV into a digital photo frame — managed from your phone.

What it is

Photo Frame is one app with two roles. On a Google TV / Android TV it displays your photos as a full-screen slideshow on the wall. On a phone or tablet it's a simple manager to add, view, and remove the photos your frame shows.

How it works

  • Photos live in a private “Photo Frame” folder in your own Google Drive. The app only ever accesses that one folder it creates — never the rest of your Drive.
  • Add photos from your phone; they appear on the TV automatically.
  • Pair a TV in seconds by scanning the on-screen QR code.
  • Prefer no account? Show photos from a local folder or USB drive instead.

Features

  • Smooth transitions (Fade, Slide, Ken Burns) and scaling modes (Fit / Fill / Stretch).
  • Adjustable interval, auto-refresh, shuffle, clock overlay, and an overnight dim schedule.
  • Starts on boot; available in English, Hebrew, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, and Russian.
  • Secure TV sign-in with a short code — no passwords typed on a remote.
