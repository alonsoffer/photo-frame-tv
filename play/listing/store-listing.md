## Full description (max 4000 chars)
Photo Frame turns your Google TV or Android TV into an elegant digital photo frame on your wall.
Point it at a folder in your Google Drive and it continuously displays your photos in a beautiful,
full-screen slideshow — perfect for family pictures, travel albums, or artwork.

Features
• Slideshow from any Google Drive folder — pick it right on the TV with the built-in folder browser.
• Smooth transitions: Fade, Slide, Ken Burns (slow zoom), or none.
• Scaling modes: Fit (letterbox), Fill (crop), or Stretch.
• Adjustable time between photos, from a few seconds to several minutes.
• Sequential or shuffled order, with optional inclusion of subfolders.
• On-screen clock overlay (12- or 24-hour).
• Overnight dim schedule so the screen isn't bright while you sleep.
• Simple remote control: left/right to change photo, OK to pause, up/menu for settings.
• Easy, secure sign-in designed for TVs — approve on your phone with a short code, no typing
  passwords with the remote.

Photo Frame requests read-only access to your Google Drive and never modifies your files.

Requires a Google account and an internet connection.
