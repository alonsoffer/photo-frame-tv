## Full description (max 4000 chars)
Photo Frame turns your Google TV or Android TV into an elegant digital photo frame on your wall,
and comes with a companion app on your phone to manage what it shows.

The same app works in two ways depending on the device:

• On your TV, it's a beautiful full-screen slideshow — perfect for family pictures, travel
  albums, or artwork on the wall.
• On your phone, it's a simple manager — add, view, and remove the photos your frame displays.

How it works
Photos live in a private "Photo Frame" folder in your own Google Drive. The app creates and
manages only that folder — it never touches the rest of your Drive. Add photos from your phone and
they appear on the TV automatically. You can also use a local folder or USB drive on the TV instead
of Drive, with no account needed.

Features
• Add photos from your phone; they show up on the TV automatically.
• Manage your frame from the phone — a gallery to view, add, and delete photos.
• Pair a TV in seconds by scanning the on-screen QR code with the phone.
• Smooth transitions: Fade, Slide, Ken Burns (slow zoom), or none.
• Scaling modes: Fit (letterbox), Fill (crop), or Stretch.
• Adjustable time between photos, auto-refresh to pick up new photos.
• Sequential or shuffled order.
• On-screen clock overlay (12- or 24-hour) and an overnight dim schedule.
• Simple remote control: left/right to change photo, OK to pause, up/menu for settings.
• Start automatically when the TV powers on.
• Available in English, Hebrew, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, and Russian.
• Easy, secure sign-in designed for TVs — approve with a short code, no typing passwords
  with the remote.
• Or skip Google entirely and show photos from a local folder or USB drive.

Photo Frame only accesses the "Photo Frame" folder it creates in your Drive, never your other
files. Requires a Google account and an internet connection for the Drive option.
