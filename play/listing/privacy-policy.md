# Privacy Policy — Photo Frame

_Last updated: 2026-07-23_

Photo Frame ("the app") is a digital photo-frame app for Google TV / Android TV that displays
photos from your Google Drive. This policy explains what the app accesses and how your data is handled.

## What the app accesses
- **Google Drive (read-only).** With your permission, the app requests the
  `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly` scope so it can list your folders and display
  the images inside the folder you choose. The app **only reads** your Drive; it never creates,
  modifies, or deletes files.

## How your data is used
- Your photos are downloaded **directly from Google's servers to your TV** solely to display them
  in the slideshow, and are cached on the device to make the slideshow smooth and reduce data use.
- Your Google sign-in (an OAuth refresh token) is stored **only on your TV**, in encrypted device
  storage, so you don't have to sign in every time.

## What we do NOT do
- We do **not** operate any server, and your photos and account data are **never sent to us** or to
  any third party. All access is between your TV and Google's own APIs.
- We do **not** collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or personal information.
- We do **not** sell or share any data.

## Your controls
- You can disconnect your Google account at any time from the app's Settings, which deletes the
  stored sign-in token from the device.
- You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at
  https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data, including the cached images and sign-in token.

## Children's privacy
The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
